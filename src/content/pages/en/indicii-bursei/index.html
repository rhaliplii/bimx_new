Exchange Indices

The Moldova International Stock Exchange will launch its first market indices as trading activity develops, once a sufficient trading period has been established to determine a representative calculation base.

Demo data

Methodology

The indices will be calculated in-house by BIMx on the basis of a public methodology approved in line with international best practice for the construction and administration of stock exchange indices. The methodology documents will be published before each index is launched.

Status

In preparation

Indices will be calculated once trading provides a sufficient basis. The methodology of each index will be published before it is launched.
