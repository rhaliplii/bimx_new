Identity

The Moldova International Stock Exchange (BIMx) is a strategic platform for capital market development, designed to facilitate access to financing and to connect the national economy to international investment flows.

Vision

A benchmark financial infrastructure

BIMx aims to become the benchmark financial infrastructure of the Republic of Moldova and a solid platform for integration with European capital markets. We promote an open market aligned with sustainable development.

Mission

Three core commitments

Access to finance

We facilitate access to the capital market for companies, municipalities and the state.

Trading framework

We offer investors a transparent, efficient and secure environment.

European integration

We help align the local market with European standards.

Extended mission — inclusion and sustainability

BIMx is building a capital market that encourages equal participation by all and supports the Republic of Moldova's transition to a green economy.

BIMx values

Six values that define us

Core principles that guide how we build the capital market.

Trust

We build lasting relationships based on integrity and respect. We provide a safe and fair environment.

Transparency

We operate under clear rules. We ensure equal access to information and accurate reporting.

Responsibility

We embrace our role as operator of critical infrastructure. Our decisions support financial stability.

Accessibility

We lower barriers to entry through financial education and technology. We are opening the market to everyone.

Innovation

We promote new products, digitalisation and regional connectivity. We continuously adapt to international markets.

Efficiency

We streamline processes. We use modern technology for fast trading and lower costs.

Governance

Modern corporate governance

BIMx applies a governance system that clearly separates the responsibilities of the Exchange Council (strategic decisions) from those of the executive team (implementation). The Council includes an independent member, and all major decisions are documented and transparent.

What this means for market participants:
Balanced decisions, not individual influence
Independent strategic oversight
Regular, accessible reporting
Rigorous compliance with the CNPF framework
