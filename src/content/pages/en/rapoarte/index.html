BIMx Reports

Once the market becomes operational, the Moldova International Stock Exchange publishes the following reports.

Daily trading report

Summary of the current session: volumes, values, number of trades and performance by instrument category. Published at the close of each session.

Monthly market bulletin

Consolidated overview of the previous month, with market commentary, segment performance and relevant corporate events. Published by the 10th of the following month.

BIMx annual report

Consolidated overview of the financial year, operational indicators and strategic developments. Published in accordance with the reporting obligations applicable to market operators.
