Real-Time Quotes

Once trading begins, the Moldova International Stock Exchange will publish market data for all instruments admitted to trading on the Regulated Market and the multilateral trading facility (MTF).

Demo data

Under Article 118 of Law No. 171/2012, companies listed on the Regulated Market publicly disclose information on their governance and activity through the Officially Appointed Mechanism for the storage of information, available here https://emitent-msi.market.md

What will be available

A unified table of admitted instruments, with filters and data displayed for each instrument.

Available filters

  • Instrument type (shares, corporate bonds, municipal bonds, government securities)
  • Market (RM / MTF)
  • Issuer's sector of activity
  • Trading currency

For each instrument

  • Symbol and issuer name
  • Reference price and last price
  • Change from the previous session
  • Volume and value traded in the current session
  • Time of last update

Market data access policy

Public

Data is published with a 15-minute delay relative to the live trading feed, in line with European practice on free access to market data.

Brokers and institutional participants

Access to real-time (undelayed) data by subscription, directly through the ARENA platform.

End of Day

The full session dataset is published free of charge at market close and remains available in the “Market Statistics” section.

Status

In preparation

Quotes will be published once trading begins, following the first listing (planned by the end of 2026).
