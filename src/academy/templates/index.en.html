Who it's for

Financial education for
the entire market

BIMx Academy programs are built around the real needs of capital market participants in the Republic of Moldova.

Investors

For anyone who wants to understand how the stock exchange works and how to make informed investment decisions.

  • Shares, bonds and stock market indices
  • Analyzing financial reports
  • Risk management and diversification

Issuers

For companies, local public authorities and the state raising capital through shares or bonds.

  • Regulated Market or Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF): specific requirements
  • Issuers listed on the Moldova Stock Exchange (BVM): how to move to BIMx
  • Municipal and government bonds
  • Reporting and transparency obligations
  • Investor relations
Admission of issuers to BIMx has been open since 28 September 2026. The first listing is planned by the end of the year.

General public

For students, professionals and anyone who wants to build a solid foundation in financial literacy.

  • The basics, explained simply
  • Glossary of stock market terms
  • Webinars and open events

Brokers and exchange traders

For investment firms going through admission to BIMx and for the exchange traders they appoint.

  • The ARENA Trading platform
  • Exchange trader certification
  • Trading simulations
  • Admission as an exchange member and settlement through the Single Central Securities Depository (DCU)
  • Initiating and Intermediary Participant
ARENA Trading training and simulations are in progress.
About BIMx

The exchange behind
BIMx Academy

The Moldova International Stock Exchange (BIMx) is the market operator authorized by the National Commission for Financial Markets (CNPF). Here is what you need to know about the institution that runs the market you are studying.

21 August 2026CNPF authorization to operate the Regulated Market and the Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF)
ARENA TradingThe trading platform, developed by the Bucharest Stock Exchange and used as SaaS
DCU · T+2Trades settle through the Single Central Securities Depository (DCU) on the second business day after the trade
10 shareholdersA public-private partnership from the Republic of Moldova and Romania, with initial share capital of MDL 29,475,000

The road to the first listing

  1. December 2025BIMx is registered
  2. June 2026Licensing application filed with CNPF
  3. 21 August 2026CNPF authorization for the Regulated Market and MTF
  4. 28 September 2026Admission of brokers and issuers begins. In parallel: connection to DCU, operating rules, ARENA Trading training, simulations
  5. 1 October 2026The ARENA platform goes live, according to the current operational schedule
  6. By the end of 2026First listing planned

FAQ

Questions about BIMx Academy?

If you can't find the answer here, write to us at office@bimx.md.

When can I start?

Right now. The lessons, quizzes, glossary and guides are open, no account needed. The full program, with registration, workshops and a certificate, starts in 2027.

Do I need prior stock market experience?

No. Beginner courses are designed for people with no prior experience and start from the basics.

What format do the courses take?

The programs will combine online modules, webinars and hands-on workshops. Each course's format is shown in its description.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, from 2027: participants who complete a course and its final assessment will receive a BIMx Academy certificate of participation. The Academy certificate does not replace exchange trader certification, which is granted only through the exam organized by BIMx.

When will I be able to buy my first shares on BIMx?

Admission of brokers and issuers began on 28 September 2026, and the first listing is planned by the end of 2026. You will trade through a broker admitted as an exchange member; the list of members is published on bimx.md.

My shares or bonds are on BVM. Do they move to BIMx automatically?

No. Admission to BIMx is a separate process that each issuer goes through; existing documents can be reused if they are still valid. Details in the lesson “Issuers listed on BVM”. Issuers can write to the Listing and Operations Department: listing@bimx.md.

I work for a broker. Where can I find ARENA Trading training?

Track 06, BIMx trading infrastructure, explains the platform, certification, simulations and admission as an exchange member. On-platform training and simulation sessions are communicated directly to brokers in the admission process.

How can I be the first to hear about the launch?

Subscribe to the BIMx newsletter at the bottom of the page to get announcements about the 2026 sessions and the opening of registration in 2027.
