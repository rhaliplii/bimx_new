Market Data

The Moldova International Stock Exchange provides institutional participants with a real-time data feed from the markets it operates.

Demo data

Real-time data feed

The real-time market data subscription is intended for broker members of the Exchange and institutional financial service providers.

Feed content

  • Trade-level price and volume
  • Order book
  • Instrument-level summary data
  • Market event alerts

Access

  • Access is provided through the ARENA platform, with secure authentication.

Public data

The public has free access to market data delayed by 15 minutes (the “Real-Time Quotes” section) and to consolidated end-of-day data (the “Market Statistics” section).
