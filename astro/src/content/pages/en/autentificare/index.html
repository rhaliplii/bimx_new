Log in

The BIMx participant portal, for exchange members, certified exchange traders and issuers admitted to trading.

The portal is not active yet

Accounts are created once the first members and issuers are admitted (admission opens on 28 September 2026). Until then, login does not work and the details you enter are not sent.

Sign in

Forgot your password? Write to us at office@bimx.md.

Who gets access

  • Exchange membersInvestment firms licensed by the CNPF and admitted as BIMx members.
  • Certified exchange tradersPeople appointed by members, after the certification procedure.
  • IssuersCompanies and authorities whose instruments are admitted to trading.

Trading takes place on the ARENA platform, using the user code and password each certified exchange trader receives.

No account?

Access is granted after admission. For questions, write to office@bimx.md.
