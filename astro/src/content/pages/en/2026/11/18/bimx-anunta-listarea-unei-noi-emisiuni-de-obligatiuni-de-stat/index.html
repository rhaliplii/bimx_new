BIMx announces the listing of a new government bond issue

A government bond issue with a total volume of MDL 210,000 has been admitted to trading on the regulated market.

Chișinău, 18 November 2026

The Moldova International Stock Exchange (BIMx) announces the admission to trading on the regulated market of an issue of government securities (GS) with a total volume of MDL 210,000. The admission is effective from 17 November 2026, following an application submitted by the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Moldova.

The issue comprises fixed-rate government bonds with a five-year term. The annual interest rate is 8.25% and the bonds mature on 13 November 2031. Interest is paid semi-annually.

Government securities are instruments through which the state raises financing from investors. Their admission to the stock exchange creates the framework for trading on the secondary market, where instruments already issued can be bought and sold between investors, including before maturity. The listing of the new issue broadens the range of financial instruments on the market operated by BIMx.

About BIMx

Moldova International Stock Exchange (BIMx) is a market operator licensed by the National Commission for Financial Markets (CNPF) on 21 August 2026 for the Regulated Market and the multilateral trading facility (MTF). Its shareholders are ten institutional entities from the Republic of Moldova and Romania; the Bucharest Stock Exchange (BVB) is a shareholder and strategic partner.
