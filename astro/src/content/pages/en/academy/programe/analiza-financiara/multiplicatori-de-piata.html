Relative valuation

Multiples relate the market price to a financial measure of the company: profit, equity, EBITDA or dividends. The idea behind relative valuation is that similar assets should trade at similar multiples. The method is quick and reflects current market conditions, but it depends critically on the choice of comparables.

Definition
Market capitalization and enterprise value

Market capitalization = share price × number of shares outstanding; it represents the market value of equity. Enterprise value (EV) = market capitalization + financial debt − cash (in simplified form); it represents the value of the whole business, for all providers of capital.

The distinction between market capitalization and EV is essential. Two companies with the same market capitalization can have very different enterprise values if one has large debts and the other holds a lot of cash. A buyer of the whole business would take on the debt but would also benefit from the existing cash; that is why EV is the right measure when comparing businesses with different financing structures. In more complete forms, EV also includes preferred shares and non-controlling interests.

The main multiples

Equity multiples
P/E = Price per share ÷ EPS; P/BV = Price per share ÷ Book value per share; Dividend yield = Dividend per share ÷ Price × 100%
P/E can be calculated on the last 12 months of earnings (trailing) or on estimated earnings (forward); do not mix the two in a comparison.
Enterprise-level multiple
EV = Market capitalization + Financial debt − Cash; EV/EBITDA = EV ÷ EBITDA
The numerator and denominator must be consistent: EV and EBITDA both relate to all providers of capital, while price and EPS relate only to shareholders.

P/EProfitable companies with stable earningsUnusable when there are losses; sensitive to non-recurring items and leverage

Example: multiples and comparables

Example
Nistru Agro SA at an illustrative price of MDL 35

Market capitalization = 35 × 2,000,000 = MDL 70,000,000 (MDL 70,000 thousand). EV = 70,000 + 22,000 − 2,300 = MDL 89,700 thousand. P/E = 35 ÷ 3.52 ≈ 9.9. P/BV = 35 ÷ 15 ≈ 2.33. EV/EBITDA = 89,700 ÷ 13,000 = 6.9. With a dividend of MDL 0.50 per share, dividend yield = 0.50 ÷ 35 ≈ 1.43%.

Applying the median P/E: implied price = 11.0 × 3.52 = MDL 38.72. Applying the median EV/EBITDA: implied EV = 7.5 × 13,000 = MDL 97,500 thousand; equity value = 97,500 − 19,700 (net debt) = MDL 77,800 thousand; per share = 77,800,000 ÷ 2,000,000 = MDL 38.90. Both values are above the illustrative price of MDL 35, but the difference must be explained before drawing any conclusion.

Tip
The median, not the mean

The median is less influenced by extreme values than the arithmetic mean, which matters when the sample of comparables is small.

The limits of multiples

Warning
A low multiple does not automatically mean a "cheap" stock

A low P/E may reflect weaker expected growth, higher risk, temporarily inflated earnings or low trading liquidity in the stock. Relative valuation only tells you how a company is priced relative to others, not whether the whole group is fairly valued. The examples are for educational purposes only and do not constitute investment recommendations.

  • Comparables should be similar in sector, size, growth, risk and accounting policies.
  • In markets with few listed companies or thin trading, prices may not reflect fundamental value.
  • Non-recurring items should be removed from earnings before calculating multiples.
  • Multiples should be cross-checked against other methods, such as DCF.

Key takeaways

  • Multiples relate price to earnings, equity, EBITDA or dividends and enable relative valuation.
  • EV = market capitalization + financial debt − cash; it is paired with firm-wide measures such as EBITDA.
  • Implied value comes from applying the comparables' median to the metrics of the company being analyzed.
  • The quality of the analysis depends on how truly comparable the companies are and on adjusting for non-recurring items.
  • A low multiple is not, on its own, an argument for investing.

Question 1 of 3A company has a market capitalization of MDL 70,000 thousand, financial debt of MDL 22,000 thousand and cash of MDL 2,300 thousand. What is its EV?

Explanation: EV = 70,000 + 22,000 − 2,300 = MDL 89,700 thousand.

Question 2 of 3Why is EV/EBITDA often preferred to P/E when comparing companies with very different levels of debt?

Explanation: EV captures the value for all providers of capital, and EBITDA is calculated before interest, so the comparison is less distorted by leverage.

Question 3 of 3The comparables' median P/E is 11.0, and the analyzed company's EPS is MDL 3.52. What is the implied price?

Explanation: Implied price = P/E × EPS = 11.0 × 3.52 = MDL 38.72.
