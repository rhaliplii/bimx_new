The Capital Market for Women Entrepreneurs

This program helps you understand every option for financing your business and the role the capital market can play in your growth strategy. We start with the classic sources — your own funds, grants, loans — and work step by step toward issuing shares or bonds. By the end, you will have a concrete action plan and a clear picture of how to prepare your business for investors.

What you will learn

  • Compare financing sources and choose the ones that fit your business's current stage
  • Understand the difference between debt and equity and how each affects control
  • Explain how share issues and corporate bond issues work, through a public offering or a private placement
  • Prepare the key documents for investors: business plan, financial model, pitch deck
  • Build transparent governance and manage dilution of control
  • Draw up a 90-day action plan for accessing financing

Capital for Women Entrepreneurs

A practical course on how to finance your business's growth, from your first savings to issuing securities. You will learn how to prepare for investors, communicate transparently, and keep your vision for the company.
