BIMx Trading Infrastructure

This program is for investment firms entering the admission process at the Moldova International Stock Exchange (BIMx), which opened on 28 September 2026, and for the people they designate as exchange traders. It explains how to work on the ARENA Trading platform, developed by the Bucharest Stock Exchange (BVB), how a trading session runs, how the certification procedure works, why trading simulations matter, and the steps to become an exchange member, including settlement through the Single Central Securities Depository (DCU). This material accompanies the training organized by BIMx and does not replace it.

What you will learn

  • Describe the BIMx operating model: what the Bucharest Stock Exchange does, what BIMx does, and what the broker does.
  • Explain the sequence of market states in a BIMx trading session and the price-time priority rule.
  • Know the requirements and stages of certification as an exchange trader, including the structure of the exam.
  • Understand the purpose of trading simulations and what to check before the first live session.
  • Go through the steps of becoming an exchange member and explain the T+2 settlement cycle through the DCU.
  • Act as an Initiating or Intermediary Participant and prepare issuers from the BVM for admission to BIMx.
