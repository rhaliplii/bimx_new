Track 01 · Beginner

Introduction to the Capital Market

This program is the starting point for anyone who wants to understand how the capital market works, with no prior knowledge of finance. You will learn what role the stock exchange plays in the economy, who the market participants are, how shares and bonds work, and what steps you take to open an account and place your first order. The explanations are simple, with illustrative numerical examples in MDL.

What you will learn

  • Explain what the capital market is and the difference between the primary and secondary markets.
  • Know what BIMx is, which markets it operates, how an order travels from the investor to the exchange, and what stage the trading launch has reached.
  • Understand how prices are formed on the exchange when buy and sell orders meet.
  • Identify the main market participants and the role of each, including the CNPF.
  • Tell shares apart from bonds and calculate basic returns: dividend, coupon, capital gain.
  • Understand the link between risk and return and why diversification and time horizon matter.
  • Know what to check when choosing a broker and how to use market, limit and stop orders.
