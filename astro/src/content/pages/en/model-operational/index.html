A market built on modern technology

The ARENA platform, developed by the BVB and operated on a SaaS basis, uses a remote-first model. Brokers connect securely from their own premises.

How it works

The operating model is distributed between the BVB, BIMx and brokers.

Component Role
BVB

Operates the platform (hosting, maintenance, know-how)
BIMx

Administers the market (rules, users, relations with the CNPF and DCU)
The broker

Distributes the market (clients, orders, connectivity)

Advantages of the model

Fast access — GUI terminal from launch

Progressive automation — Gateway API in year 2, FIX in year 3

Real-time market data

T+2 settlement through the Central Securities Depository

Technology roadmap

Phase Components
Launch (2026)

GUI terminal, DCU integration, training
Year 2

Gateway API, DataFeed
Year 3

FIX Engine, CSD Realtime
Year 4+

Scaling, new products
