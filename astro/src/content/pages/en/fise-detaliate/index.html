Detailed instrument fact sheets

Every instrument admitted to trading on the BIMx markets will have a public fact sheet bringing together its identification details, trading data and the issuer's reporting documents.

Demo data

What a detailed fact sheet contains

Identification details

Issuer name, trading symbol, ISIN code, market of admission (RM / MTF), listing category, trading currency.

Trading data

Reference price, opening price, session low / high, last price, volume and value traded, market capitalisation.

Historical chart

Price and volume performance over selectable periods (day, week, month, year).

Issuer information

Full name, registered office, IDNO code, share capital, shareholder structure (to the extent required by disclosure obligations), official website.

Documents and reports

Annual and half-yearly financial reports, audit reports, resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders, announcements of corporate events.

Status

In preparation

Detailed factsheets go live once the first instruments are admitted to trading.
