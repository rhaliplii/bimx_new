Council and Executive Body

Executive Management

The executive team is responsible for the strategy and day-to-day operations of the Moldova International Stock Exchange. It puts the vision and strategic decisions into practice, turning BIMx’s mission into functional processes, accessible products and an efficient trading framework. The executive team responsible for the exchange’s strategy and operations.

BIMx Board

The BIMx Board is responsible for the strategic decisions of the Moldova International Stock Exchange. It comprises shareholder representatives and one independent member, in line with the shareholding structure. Together, the Board members bring diverse expertise in finance, institutions and governance to guide the development of the capital market. The strategic decisions of the Moldova International Stock Exchange are taken by the BIMx Board, made up of shareholder representatives and one independent member, arranged according to the shareholding structure.

Alexandru Sonic

Chair of the Board / Credit Sector

Alexandru Sonic is Chair of the Board and represents investors from the financial sector. As Deputy Chief Executive of maib, responsible for the corporate segment, he brings extensive experience in investment, financial markets, corporate governance and risk management, gained both at leading international banking institutions and in the market of the Republic of Moldova. His expertise helps strengthen a strategic vision focused on the sustainable development of the capital market, greater investor confidence and alignment with international standards of governance and transparency.
